**轻透截图 0.9.6：智能抠图与截图体验升级。** 本版整合了用户提供的 0.9.5 增强，并修正大图宽框下的细线漏检。

**QingTou 0.9.6: guided cutouts and better capture tools.** Integrates enhancements from the user-provided 0.9.5 source and improves thin-detail recovery in large, loose subject boxes.

## 下载与更新 / Download and update

下载本 Release 附件中的 **QingTou.exe**，双击即可运行，无需安装。更新前从托盘退出旧版，再替换 EXE；保留原来的 `QingTou.ini` 可继续使用已有设置。

Download **QingTou.exe** from this release's assets and run it directly. Exit the old tray instance before replacing it; retain `QingTou.ini` to keep settings.

完整便携 ZIP 另含使用指南、许可证和第三方声明；源码 ZIP 供开发者编译使用。`SHA256SUMS.txt` 用于校验附件。

The full portable ZIP includes guides and licensing. The source ZIP is for developers. Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify downloads.

## 新功能 / Features

- 框选智能抠图、保留 / 去除画笔、边缘精修、原图对照、重置与撤销。
- 鼠标悬停预选窗口，单击截图；空白桌面回退到显示器，并保留自由拖框。
- 8–144 px 可编辑文字、完整 RGB 色板、4–64 px 马赛克块大小。
- 按下立即显示的增量画笔、按变化行刷新画面、魔棒可选跟随渐变。
- Guided subject cutouts with correction brushes, edge refinement, original comparison, reset, and undo.
- Window/monitor preselection alongside free region dragging.
- Editable 8–144 px text, arbitrary RGB colors, 4–64 px mosaic blocks, incremental pen previews, and optional gradient following.

## 本次额外修正 / Additional fixes

- 在可靠的平滑渐变背景上，大图分割会保留网格内的强前景证据，减少细线因仅采样中心而漏检。
- 在原图分辨率恢复背景，避免细线膨胀为粗网格块；保留去除标记优先级。
- 新增 2560×1440 宽框细线回归用例，保留 601/601 个线条像素、额外背景像素为 0。也覆盖单像素主体和空渐变背景。这些是合成测试结果，不代表任意图片都能完全自动抠图。
- Preserve strong foreground evidence inside coarse cells on reliable smooth gradients, then remove background at original resolution.
- A synthetic 2560×1440 loose-box regression retains 601/601 line pixels with zero extra background pixels; single-pixel subjects and empty gradients are also covered. Results do not guarantee automatic extraction for every image.

## 使用边界 / Limitations

智能抠图全程本机计算，松手后更新预览。复杂纹理、同色前景、发丝等仍可能需要框紧主体并用画笔修补；大图响应、HDR、混合 DPI 多屏和远程桌面仍需更多实机验证。

Processing is local and previews update after release. Complex textures, similar foreground/background colors, and hair may require tighter boxes and corrections. Large-image responsiveness, HDR, mixed-DPI displays, and remote desktop need broader desktop validation.
