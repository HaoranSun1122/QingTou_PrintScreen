截图后直接提取 Logo、图标，导出无背景透明 PNG，方便制作 PPT、文档和设计排版。原生 Windows 单文件程序，当前 x64 EXE 约 **1.5 MiB**，图像处理在本机完成。

## 本次更新

- **新增智能抠图**：框住主体即可生成透明预览，配合「保留画笔」「去除画笔」修补，支持边缘精修、查看原图、重置和撤销。
- **新增窗口预选**：鼠标悬停高亮窗口，单击确认；桌面空白处可预选显示器，也保留自由拖框截图。
- **标注更灵活**：文字支持 8–144 px 调整与再次编辑，颜色支持完整 RGB 色板，马赛克块大小可在 4–64 px 之间调整。
- **画笔反馈更及时**：按下即可显示笔迹，拖动时增量绘制并局部刷新；魔棒抠图新增可选的「跟随渐变」。
- **改善细小内容保留**：减少大图、宽松框选场景下细线和小主体被漏掉的问题，同时避免细线周围残留成块背景。

原有的圆形拖选、多次累积、减选、透明 PNG 保存与复制、收紧到选区及 GIF 录制继续保留。

## 使用提示

智能抠图建议先框住完整主体，并在周围留少量背景；有遗漏时用保留 / 去除画笔修补，应用后点「收紧到选区」裁掉透明空白。

本版为预览版。复杂纹理、前景与背景颜色接近、发丝等场景仍可能需要手动修补；大图响应、HDR、混合 DPI 多屏和远程桌面仍需更多实机验证。

## QingTou 0.9.6 Preview

Capture a logo or icon and export a background-free transparent PNG for presentations, documents, and design layouts. The current native Windows x64 executable is about **1.5 MiB**, and image processing stays on your computer.

### Download and update

Download **`QingTou.exe`** from **Assets** below and double-click to run. No installation or compilation is needed. The app interface is currently Chinese; bilingual guides are included in the portable ZIP.

- **`QingTou.exe`**: the standalone app for everyday use.
- **`QingTou-v0.9.6-windows-x64.zip`**: the full portable package with guides, licenses, and third-party notices.
- **`QingTou-v0.9.6-source.zip`**: source for developers. GitHub's automatic **Source code** archives also require compilation.
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`**: checksums for the downloads.

Supports SDR desktops on **Windows 10 version 2004+ / Windows 11 x64**. Exit the old tray instance before replacing the EXE. Keep `QingTou.ini` to retain settings.

### What's new

- **Guided cutouts**: box a subject, preview transparency, and correct with Keep/Remove brushes. Includes edge refinement, original comparison, reset, and undo.
- **Window preselection**: hover to highlight a window, then click to capture. Empty desktop areas fall back to the monitor; free region dragging remains available.
- **More flexible annotations**: editable 8–144 px text, arbitrary RGB colors, and 4–64 px mosaic blocks.
- **More immediate pen feedback**: ink appears on mouse-down, with incremental drawing and partial repainting. Color-based cutouts also gain an optional gradient-following mode.
- **Better thin-detail recovery**: reduces missed thin lines and small subjects in large, loosely framed images while avoiding coarse background blocks around recovered lines.

Existing circular selection brushes, additive/subtractive selection, transparent PNG saving and copying, trim-to-content, and GIF recording remain available.

### Tips and limitations

Frame the whole subject with a little surrounding background. Correct the preview with Keep/Remove brushes, apply the result, then trim to content to remove empty canvas.

This is a preview release. Complex textures, similar foreground/background colors, and hair may need manual corrections. Large-image responsiveness, HDR, mixed-DPI displays, and remote desktop need broader desktop validation.
